About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Indiana University Bloomington, advised by Prof. Haixu Tang. Previously, I received an M.Sc. in Information Technology from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology and a B.Eng. in Communication Engineering from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China.

My research lies at the intersection of time series forecasting and AI for healthcare. I aim to build forecasting models that are accurate, efficient, and reliable, and to apply them to real-world clinical data, with the broader goal of developing trustworthy foundation models for healthcare.

Earlier in my Ph.D., I worked on the security and privacy of real-world systems, ranging from mobile software supply chains to robotic vehicles, with publications at ACM CCS, USENIX Security, and ICRA. I am always happy to discuss research and potential collaborations — feel free to reach out.
